A collection of customizable clock and date widgets for displaying your system time on your MoBro dashboard. Customize fonts, colors, time formats, and alignment to perfectly match your dashboard layout.

#### Digital Clock

A digital clock widget that displays your system time in real time.
Choose between 12-hour and 24-hour formats, optionally hide seconds, suppress the AM/PM suffix, and remove the leading zero from the hour for a cleaner look.
Fully styleable with custom fonts, font sizes, text color, and both horizontal and vertical alignment controls.

#### Digital Date

A digital date widget that shows your system date without the time component.
Select the date order that suits your region — day-month-year, year-month-day, or month-day-year — and choose your preferred separator character (dot, dash, or slash).
Supports the same styling options as the clock widget for a consistent appearance across your dashboard.
